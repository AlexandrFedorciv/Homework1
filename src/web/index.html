How PartsBin works

01

Tell it what you have

Type your parts (4x sg90, servo x4 and servo (4) all work) or drop a photo of your parts bin; a vision model lists what it sees and you fix the list.

02

Add constraints

Goal, skill level, time and budget are all optional. Tick "I can't solder" for breadboard-only builds.

03

AI designs, code checks

The model proposes three projects as structured JSON. The app — not the AI — works out what you own, what's missing and what it costs, and verifies every constraint.

04

Fix what breaks

Projects that are too long, too hard or over budget are sent back with the reason and replaced. You get the best three, a picture of the parts and a shopping list.

Reading a project card

  • ✓ you own it · ◐ you own some, need more · ✗ missing (with an indicative price in CZK)
  • The picture shows every part: solid = yours, dashed = to buy.
  • The chips at the bottom are the constraint checks the app ran on that project.
  • ⬇ Shopping list downloads everything you'd need to buy as a Markdown checklist.
